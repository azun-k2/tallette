The short version

Tallette collects no data. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you type can leave your device. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics.

Your tapes stay on your device

Your tapes, their comments and their totals are kept in the files you save on your Mac, or in the app's own folder in the Files app on iPhone and iPad. Exports and imports read and write only the files you choose. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

The app has no network permission at all. Currencies are shown as symbols; there are no exchange rates to fetch.
